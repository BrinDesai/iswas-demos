Fabric notes

Know your cloth.

Three techniques behind the collection — the slow work that makes each piece.

01

Boro

Patchwork inspired by the Japanese art of repairing textiles — offcuts given a second life in 100% cotton.

02

Quilting

Fine silk, lightly padded, layered and hand-quilted, then washed for softness and texture.

03

Hand screen print

Every print pulled by hand. It stiffens the silk and marks each piece as made, not manufactured.
